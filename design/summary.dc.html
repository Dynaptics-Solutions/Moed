A planner that tells you when your day is full

That is the whole idea. Everything below follows from it.

The problem

Then you fail, the list turns red, and you stop trusting it. Nobody's app stops you. They just count tasks.

Five kinds of thing you can add

Each one behaves differently. That is the only reason we have kinds at all.

Task
One thing, one length. "Rewrite the onboarding copy, 2 hours." This is most things.
Routine
A checklist that repeats. "Morning routine" is six ticks inside one 40 minute block. It costs the day 40 minutes, not six entries.
Session
Time, not a tick box. "Reading, 1 hour." You cannot finish it, you can only give it time.
Errand
A place, not a time. Groceries, post office and pharmacy become one 40 minute trip instead of three loose tasks.
Appointment
Fixed. You cannot move it. It comes from your calendar. You can only add travel time and prep around it.
Nobody picks a kind from a menu.
You type one line and get a Task, because that is what most things are. Kinds only appear when you choose a template, or tap "make this a routine" on something you already wrote.
Meals, money, bills, events

You asked about these. Here is the rule that keeps them from turning the app into a mess.

Everything you plan spends one of two things. Hours, or money.
A day has a limit in hours. A week has a limit in money. Same screen, same bar, different unit. If a feature does not spend hours or money, it does not go in.
Yes
Bills
The best fit of the lot. A bill is to money what an appointment is to time: fixed, dated, cannot be moved. It lowers the week's limit the moment you add it, and "leave by" becomes "pay by".
Yes
Spending
A weekly limit and a bar, not a ledger. You set a cap, log a line, see what is left. No bank sync, no receipt scanning — that is a finance app, not this.
Yes
Meals
Dinner is a Routine with a shopping list attached. Plan the week and you get five 40 minute blocks and one errand run. No recipe database — that is somebody else's product.
No
Event planning
You already have it. An event is a project with a date and a budget — add those two fields to Projects and a birthday, a launch and a trip are the same thing. Guest lists and RSVPs are a different product.

Free or paid

The most common complaint about planner apps is that the paywall appears after you have already moved in. So the line has to be one you can explain in a sentence.

The rule
If it costs us money every month, it is paid. If it is just software, it is free.
Servers, AI and map lookups have a real bill attached to each user. The day limit, the tray and the evening close do not — they run on your phone. So they are free, and they stay free.

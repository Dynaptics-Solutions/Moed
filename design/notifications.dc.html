The rule that keeps this from becoming noise
One scheduled nudge a day — the evening close. Everything else is either something you asked for (a reminder you set on a record) or a consequence of something you just did (you went over the limit, your watch closed a Session). There is no morning nudge: the evening close already showed you tomorrow. The one exemption is the trial reminder on day thirteen, which is about the money and fires once. Nothing fires to re-engage you, nothing fires twice, and every one of them can be answered without opening the app. If a notification cannot carry its own decision, it should not be sent.
